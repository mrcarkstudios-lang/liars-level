## Full description (max 4000)
Welcome! Nothing here will hurt you. (That was a lie.)

Liar's Level is a short, brutal and funny troll platformer. The signs lie. The floor lies. Even the exit door lies. Die once, learn the trick, and never fall for it again... until the next one.

HOW IT PLAYS
- Run, jump and reach the door. That's it. Until the door runs away.
- Every trap has a tell. Spot it, and the lie stops working.
- Instant respawn in under half a second. "One more try" is one tap.
- Levels are tiny: 5-10 seconds when you know the trick, a lifetime when you don't.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- 50 handmade levels in 5 worlds: Trust Issues, Crumbling Promises, Betrayal, Chaos Theory and Total Lies. No level repeats, and every one is harder than the one before.
- Hidden spikes, fake doors, crumbling and shy floors, ceiling blocks, invisible walls, runaway exits, buzzsaws, springs, moving platforms, rising lava, gravity pads and a fake "LEVEL CLEARED" screen.
- The Liar: a smug narrator who taunts you on every death and reacts to how you play.
- Ghost race: your best run is replayed as a ghost so you can beat yourself.
- Daily Lie: a new challenge every day with a streak counter.
- 6 hero skins to unlock, rank titles from "Gullible" to "Master of Lies", and a brag button to share your score.
- Pixel-art look with a custom soundtrack for every world.
- Touch controls built for phones, plus gamepad and keyboard support.

Stuck? After a few deaths the Liar offers a hint, and after many it lets you skip the level (optional ads).

Free to play. Optional rewarded ads help you skip or get hints, and a one-time purchase removes full-screen ads.

Think you can't be fooled? The Liar says: "Trust me."
